drupal/* resolves against packages.drupal.org/8; everything else resolves against repo.packagist.org.

A verdict of unverifiable means the requirement could not be read, not that the module is broken. It is reported separately from blocked.

Installing fetches the package and writes its files. Enabling is the separate step that turns the module on, and it reboots the interpreter.
