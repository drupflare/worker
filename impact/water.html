Water

Water figures for Drupflare and the hosts it is compared with.

derived from the same energy totals, using 0.375 L/kWh direct and about 4.55 L/kWh indirect: US data centres used 176 TWh in 2023, with 66 billion litres consumed directly and nearly 800 billion indirectly through generation [16]. With E the annual energy in kWh and W the water in litres a year:

W_{\text{direct}} = 0.375 \cdot E
W_{\text{indirect}} = 4.55 \cdot E

Water does not total the way carbon does. Carbon is global and fungible. Water is local: the same volume saved in a water-stressed watershed and in a wet one are not equivalent, and aggregate litres carry no information about which.
