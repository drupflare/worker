Impact

What Drupflare costs to run in electricity, carbon, water and money, and how far each figure can be trusted. This page holds the labels every figure carries and a map of the pages that hold the figures.

Contents
  1. Pages
  2. Measurement Labels

Pages

  • What This Does Not Claim: the limits of every claim.
  • Reproducing The Numbers: commands that reproduce each figure.
  • References: the numbered references the other pages cite.
  • Energy: the saving against conventional hosting, operating states and the per-request model.
  • Energy Per View: the energy of one view, the traffic mix, visitor devices and Spool.
  • Fleet Energy: many sites at once, institutional workloads and the saving by site count.
  • Carbon, Water and Embodied Carbon: the environmental figures.
  • Economics: what each host costs at each traffic level, the plan limits and the comparison against a VPS.

Measurement Labels

Every figure on the impact pages carries one of four labels.

label meaning
measured read off an instrument on real hardware, with an n and a spread
derived arithmetic over measured inputs and published constants
derived (modelled) arithmetic that also takes an input nobody has measured for a real site
published quoted from a named external source, cited where it appears

A figure keeps its label wherever it is repeated.
